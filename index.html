A note before you begin.

from the researcher

I started this project because I wanted to move beyond guessing about how autonomous agents respond to different defensive measures.

I do not know whether the approaches in this prototype will make a meaningful difference, and I do not want to present them as proven defenses.

If the results are weak or inconclusive, that is still useful.

It may show which ideas do not justify further effort, or what a better study needs to control.

If some approaches appear promising, that would be a reason to test them more carefully, not proof that they work reliably in real systems.

Either way, I hope the project can contribute a small piece of evidence and help shape better questions for future research.

I cannot offer compensation, and taking part will cost you time and tokens.

What I can offer is a chance to contribute to better evidence on how autonomous agents behave against different defenses, which matters if you are concerned about where this capability is heading.
